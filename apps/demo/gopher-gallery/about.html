Our Story

Or: how we learned to stop weeding and love the gopher.

Founded in 2023 after our backyard garden was mysteriously perfect (turns out the gophers were doing landscaping), Gopher Gallery decided these underrated diggers deserved a spotlight — and a price tag.

It started with a single unexplained mound near the tomato bed. Then a suspiciously well-aerated flower border. Then a network of tunnels so architecturally sound our contractor asked who did the excavation work. The answer, embarrassingly, was: nobody we'd hired.

We caught our first glimpse of the culprit at dusk — a stout, whiskered little face poking out of a perfectly circular hole, clearly proud of a job well done. We were charmed. We were humbled. We were, frankly, out-landscaped by a rodent.

Rather than call pest control, we called a family meeting. If gophers were going to be this good at their jobs, the least we could do was give them proper credit, better lodging, and — let's be honest — really cute name tags. Gopher Gallery was born: a boutique shop dedicated to the idea that the world's most underrated burrower deserves to be somebody's favorite pet.

Today, we hand-raise and rehome gophers of every species — Pocket, Plains, Botta's, and the imposing Giant — matching each one with a family ready to embrace the chaos, the charm, and the occasional unexpected hole in the lawn.

Our mission is simple: give these tunnel-visioned little landscapers the spotlight (and the belly rubs) they deserve.
