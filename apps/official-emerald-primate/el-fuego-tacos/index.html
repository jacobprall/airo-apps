Our Story

Marisol Reyes started this cart back in 2014 with two things: her grandmother's salsa recipes, written on index cards stained with years of cooking, and a single griddle she bought used from a diner going out of business.

She parked at 5th & Main because it was close to home and the light was good for the morning prep. Word got around fast — turns out a fresh tortilla and a salsa verde made from scratch will do that.

Twelve years later, El Fuego is a neighborhood fixture. Same corner, same fire, same recipes. We know our regulars by name and their order by heart. ¡Órale, pull up — we're parked and ready!
