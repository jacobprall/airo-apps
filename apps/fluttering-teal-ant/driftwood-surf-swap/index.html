How it works

Selling a board shouldn't feel like unloading a used car. Driftwood is just a corkboard for surfers — post your stick, chat with a local buyer, meet up, trade cash for board. That's it.

1

List it

Snap a few photos, be honest about dings, price it fair. Five minutes, no fees.

2

Talk it out

Buyers reach out directly. Answer questions, agree on a spot to meet — a beach parking lot works great.

3

Swap and surf

Check the board in person, hand over cash, shake hands. Somebody's garage board is now back in the water.

Buying safely: meet in daylight, check for delam or soft spots, and always test-fit under your arm before you pay. If a deal feels off, it probably is — trust your gut, not just the glide.
